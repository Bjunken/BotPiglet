BotPiglet Privacy Policy

BotPiglet is a private tool used only by its owner.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, BotPiglet uses read-only Google Classroom access to see the owner's courses, announcements, coursework and course materials.

How it is used

The data is used only to post notifications about new Classroom items to the owner's own Discord server. BotPiglet stores the OAuth token and the IDs of items it has already posted on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
